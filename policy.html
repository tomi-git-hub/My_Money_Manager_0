App: My Money Manager
Last updated: 29 October 2026

Part 1: Privacy Policy

What we save

Your email address. Your loan records: names of people, amounts, dates, notes, payments, payment methods, payment screenshots, and reminders.

Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The app owner cannot see it.

Why we save it

Only to run the app and show you your own records. There are no ads. We do not sell or share your data.

Where it is kept

On Google Firebase servers (Realtime Database, Singapore region). Security rules let each person read and write only their own records. The app owner can technically access stored data through the Firebase console, and will not look at it except to fix a problem.

Other people's information

You may type names of friends or relatives. Please only enter what you are comfortable saving, and respect their privacy.

Your control

You can edit and delete your loans, payments and reminders inside the app. For deleting your whole account, contact us. We will try to help, but we cannot promise it. Backup files you download stay on your own phone and are your responsibility.

Age

This app is for people aged 18 and above.
